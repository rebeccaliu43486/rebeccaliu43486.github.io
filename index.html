Rebecca Jiaxiu Liu, Coauthor Name

    • Built bank balance-sheet, lending and securitisation data for Buchak et al. (2024).
    • Analysed the diffusion of technology with patent filing, citation, inventor, Capital IQ M&A and text-similarity data; linked Bank Call Reports to Capital IQ to bring bank types into the assignee analysis; and extended the patent assignee industry classification for Lerner et al. (2024).
    • Digitised and crosswalked eight decades of the historical US manufacturing census and integrated NHGIS population data; estimated PPML gravity models of inventor flows on county-to-county networks.
    • Designed an LLM pipeline for OCR to build a new dataset of university board members from 1980 to 2024, and matched it to Revelio Labs and DIME data.
    • Ran difference-in-differences event studies on options data linking market volatility to elections, for Denes et al. (2017).
    • Used LLM keyword search, accounting identities and econometric models to back out aircraft model-year transaction prices from 10-K filings, for Franks et al. (2022).
    • Cleaned and crosswalked ten years of newly digitised UN trade-flow data in Stata, for regression and policy analysis.
    • Summarised three decades of Singapore’s industrial policies from the academic literature and government archives, for Juhász et al. (2024).
    • Trained text-analysis models to identify industrial policies, for Juhász et al. (2025), and turned policy documents into labelled training data: 3-digit ISIC sectors, policy goals, and instruments such as subsidies, tariffs and R&D support.
    • Analysed post-Brexit trends in stocks, derivatives, IPOs and security clearing with NYSE, Euronext and LSEG data.
    • Acknowledged contributor to Atlas of Finance: Mapping the Global Story of Money (Yale University Press, 2024).
    • Designed 3D Kagome lattice structures and ran stress tests, using statistical modelling to infer their mechanical properties.
